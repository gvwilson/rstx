### Key design decision

The heatwall grid was redesigned during implementation.  An initial harmonic-mean approach
produced incorrect fluxes because the harmonic mean is only exact when a material interface
lies at the midpoint between two nodes.  The final design stores conductivity per segment
(k_seg) rather than per node; because every inter-node segment lies entirely within one
material, each segment's conductivity is unambiguous and the FD scheme recovers the exact
piecewise-linear solution to machine precision.
